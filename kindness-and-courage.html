Chapter IV · Documentary photographs

Kindness
& Courage

When life had very little to give, people still found something to give each other.

Kindness

Kindness

In a farming community that had very little, neighbours brought whatever they could cook and served one another at a long table.

Nobody here is rich, yet the table is full. Kindness is often just this — passing the plate along.

Russell Lee · 1940

Family

Family

A mother holds her baby; the eldest son carries the family’s belongings on his back while his little sister clings to his hand.

They have just arrived in a new country with everything they own in a few bags. What they carried most was each other.

Lewis Hine · 1905

Courage

Courage

A father and his two sons cross their farm in a dust storm; the youngest shields his face. The fence posts are almost buried.

Still, they keep walking. Hard times are not only endured — they are walked through, one step at a time.

Arthur Rothstein · 1936

Childhood

Childhood

A small girl stands between towering spinning machines, looking straight at us.

Hine’s photographs of children at work helped change the laws that allowed it. A single honest picture can make a whole society stop and look.

Lewis Hine · 1908

Dignity

Dignity

A worker leans into a giant steam pump, his body curving with the machine. Hine called these his “work portraits”.

They remind me that ordinary labour has its own strength and grace — and the people who do it deserve to be seen.

Lewis Hine · 1920

Old society

Old society

A narrow alley, laundry overhead, faces staring back at the camera.

Riis showed comfortable readers how the poorest families lived, and his pictures helped push for better housing. More than a century later, those faces still ask us not to look away.

Jacob Riis · 1888

In the end

None of these people expected to be remembered. They were simply living through hard times — sharing food, holding hands, walking into the wind. That is why their pictures still matter: they show that kindness and courage are, most of the time, ordinary things.
